How it plays

Not a chatbot. A game with rules.

Real D&D-style mechanics run under every scene — so your decisions have weight, and the world pushes back.

Click to act

No typing into a void. The DM offers handcrafted action cards each turn — you choose, the story moves.

The dice decide

A d20 against your ability modifiers settles every risk. Watch it roll, then live with the result.

Failure opens doors

A miss never dead-ends. The story branches into new choices — sometimes worse, sometimes wilder.

Turn-based combat

Enemies have stats, armor, and their own dice. Trade blows, read weaknesses, or flee for your life.

Loot that matters

The AI forges items with real mechanical bonuses — a blade that bites harder, an amulet that sharpens your mind.

Quests that remember

Side quests live in your sidebar and stay in the DM's memory, woven back into the tale when you least expect.
