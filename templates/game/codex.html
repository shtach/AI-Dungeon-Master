Tomb Wraith

Creature · Undead
AC 13 18 HP ATK +4 · 1d8 The lower crypt

A chill that walks. Tomb wraiths are the lingering grief of those buried without rites — bound to the stone that holds their bones, hungry for the warmth of the living.

They are slow to anger but relentless once roused, and their touch leaches the heat from flesh and courage alike. Fire and consecrated steel are said to be the only things they truly fear.

The Village Elder

Character · NPC
Stonehollow Quest giver

The elder of Stonehollow has watched over the village for longer than anyone can remember. His voice is steady, but his eyes carry the weight of the missing — those who walked into the dark and never returned.

The Tomb of Silent Kings

Place · Dungeon
Stonehollow outskirts Dangerous

Beneath the hills outside Stonehollow lies a network of catacombs once used to inter the kings of a forgotten dynasty. The entrance collapsed generations ago, but the silence inside has never been broken — until now.

The great war ended when the coalition of mortal kingdoms stormed the Demon King's citadel and sealed the rift he had torn between worlds. The victory was real, but it cost a generation. Every village in the Heartlands lost someone.
